Manual playlists: next to smart (rule-based) playlists, make playlists you fill by hand, like extra queues. Add episodes with "Add to playlist…" from any episode's menu or multi-select, drag to reorder, swipe to remove, play from any episode. Played episodes drop off unless you keep them. A smart playlist can be copied into a manual one. Icons show each playlist's kind. The queue is unchanged.
